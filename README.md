Risk Management Plan (RMP) in Pharmacovigilance

Project Overview

This academic project explores the role of Risk Management Plans (RMPs) in pharmacovigilance and their importance in ensuring the safe use of medicines.

Objectives

- Understand the concept and importance of RMPs.
- Identify known risks, potential risks, and missing safety information.
- Understand pharmacovigilance activities.
- Study routine and additional risk minimization measures.
- Understand the importance of continuous drug safety monitoring.

Key Topics

1. Introduction to Pharmacovigilance
2. Objectives of a Risk Management Plan
3. Components of an RMP
4. Safety Specification
5. Pharmacovigilance Plan
6. Risk Minimization Measures
7. Evaluation of Risk Minimization Effectiveness
8. Regulatory Framework: EMA, CDSCO, and PvPI

Project Type

Academic Assignment | Online Pharmacovigilance Internship

Learning Outcome

This project provides an introduction to drug safety monitoring, adverse drug reactions, risk assessment, and strategies for minimizing medicine-related risks.

References

- European Medicines Agency (EMA) — Good Pharmacovigilance Practices, Module V
- Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), India
- Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC) — Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI)
